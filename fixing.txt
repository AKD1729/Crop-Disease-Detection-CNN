  Great observation — this is a fundamental flaw in the project's design, not just a minor bug. Here's the breakdown:                      
  ──────
  ### Why the Model Predicted Your Photo as "Rice Healthy" at 95%+                                                                         
                                                                                                                                           
  Root cause: Closed-set classification with softmax.                                                                                      
                                                                                                                                           
  The model has exactly 10 classes and uses softmax, which forces all probabilities to sum to 1.0. It must assign every input to one of the
  10 classes — there is no "none of the above" option. So when you feed it a photo of a person, a car, or a pizza, it will still           
  confidently pick one of the 10 crop disease classes.                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                           
  Why high confidence on garbage input? Softmax doesn't measure "is this input something I've seen before?" — it only measures "among my 10
  known classes, which logit is largest?". If one logit is even slightly dominant over the others, softmax amplifies it into a high        
  probability. Neural networks are notoriously overconfident on out-of-distribution (OOD) data.                                            
                                                                                                                                           
  Why "Rice Healthy" specifically? Likely because:                                                                                         
                                                                                                                                           
  • Healthy leaf classes have more visual diversity (varied backgrounds, lighting) compared to disease classes with specific patterns
  • Your photo may share low-level features (color tones, textures) that the model loosely associates with healthy leaves                  
  • The model may have learned background/shortcut features rather than true leaf features — exactly the risk flagged in the plan's        
  Limitation #1                                                                                                                            
  ──────                                                                                                                                   
  ### Why the 60% Fallback Failed                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                           
  The plan's confidence threshold mechanism (Module 8) is architecturally incapable of catching this scenario:                             
                                                                                                                                           
   What the threshold detects                                           │ What it doesn't detect
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Ambiguity between known classes (e.g., two diseases look similar →   │ Completely irrelevant inputs (OOD) that the model has never seen
   split confidence)                                                    │
                                                                                                                                           
  A softmax score of 95% on your photo doesn't mean the model is 95% sure it's rice — it means among the 10 classes, one logit dominated.  
  The threshold only works when the model is genuinely confused between its known classes, not when it encounters something entirely
  outside its training domain.                                                                                                             
  ──────                                                                                                                                   
  ### What's Missing (Design Gap)                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                           
  To properly handle this, the project would need one or more of:                                                                          
                                                                                                                                           
  1. An OOD / rejection mechanism — e.g., an additional "unknown/not-a-leaf" class trained on negative examples                            
  2. Input validation — a separate lightweight classifier or heuristic to verify the image is actually a leaf before passing to the disease
  model                                                                                                                                    
  3. Calibration — techniques like temperature scaling to make softmax scores more meaningful                                              
  4. Embedding-based rejection — compare the input's feature vector distance to known training clusters; reject if too far                 
                                                                                                                                           
  None of these were in scope in the plan (and none are in the code), but the plan also doesn't acknowledge this as a limitation. The 8    
  listed limitations mention narrow disease coverage and shortcut learning, but never mention that non-plant images will be confidently    
  misclassified — which is arguably the most user-facing failure mode.

  Here are the fixes, ordered from simplest to most robust, with implementation approach for each:                                         
  ──────                                                                                                                                   
  ## Fix 1 — Input Validation Gate (Easiest, No Retraining)                                                                                
                                                                                                                                           
  Idea: Add a pre-check before the disease model — verify the image is actually a leaf.                                                    
                                                                                                                                           
  How:                                                                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                           
    Upload → Is it a leaf? → YES → Disease CNN → Prediction                                                                                
                           → NO  → "Please upload a leaf image"                                                                            
                                                                                                                                           
  Two options:
                                                                                                                                           
  A) Color heuristic (quick & dirty)                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                           
  • Check if the image has sufficient green-channel dominance (leaves are predominantly green)                                             
  • Compute the green ratio in HSV color space; reject if below a threshold                                                                
  • Pros: Zero retraining, fast, simple                                                                                                    
  • Cons: Fails on brown/yellow diseased leaves, fails on green non-leaf objects                                                           
                                                                                                                                           
  B) Pretrained classifier gate (more reliable)                                                                                            
                                                                                                                                           
  • Use a lightweight pretrained model (e.g., MobileNet) to classify "leaf vs not-leaf"                                                    
  • Only pass images classified as leaves to the disease CNN                                                                               
  • Pros: Much more accurate rejection                                                                                                     
  • Cons: Adds a second model to deploy, needs a small labeled dataset of leaf/non-leaf images                                             
  ──────
  ## Fix 2 — Add an 11th "Unknown" Class (Requires Retraining)                                                                             
                                                                                                                                           
  Idea: Add random non-leaf images as a dedicated "Unknown" class during training.                                                         
                                                                                                                                           
  How:                                                                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                           
  • Collect ~500-1000 random images (people, objects, animals, textures) — easy to source from datasets like ImageNet samples or COCO      
  • Add them as class 11: "Unknown / Not a Crop Leaf"                                                                                      
  • Retrain the model with 11 classes                                                                                                      
  • Update CLASSES list and num_classes=11 in app.py                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                           
  Pros: Single model, simple concept, directly addresses the problem                                                                       
  Cons: Can't cover every possible OOD input — the "Unknown" class would need to be diverse enough. May slightly reduce accuracy on the    
  original 10 classes.                                                                                                                     
  ──────
  ## Fix 3 — Entropy-Based Rejection (No Retraining)                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                           
  Idea: Replace the max-confidence threshold with prediction entropy — a better uncertainty measure.                                       
                                                                                                                                           
  How:                                                                                                                                     
  Instead of:                                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                           
    # Current: check if max probability > 60%                                                                                              
    conf, pred_idx = torch.max(probabilities, dim=0)                                                                                       
    if conf >= 0.60: ...                                                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                           
  Use:                                                                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                           
    # Entropy: how spread out is the probability distribution?                                                                             
    entropy = -torch.sum(probabilities * torch.log(probabilities + 1e-9))
    max_entropy = torch.log(torch.tensor(10.0))  # max possible for 10 classes                                                             
    normalized_entropy = entropy / max_entropy                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                           
    if normalized_entropy > threshold:  # e.g., 0.3                                                                                        
        # Uncertain → reject                                                                                                               
                                                                                                                                           
  Why it's better than max-confidence: OOD inputs sometimes produce unusual softmax distributions (e.g., one spike + near-zeros) that      
  entropy can catch better than raw max probability.                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                           
  Cons: Still not reliable — neural networks can produce low-entropy (confident) outputs on OOD data too. This alone won't fully solve the 
  problem.                                                                                                                                 
  ──────                                                                                                                                   
  ## Fix 4 — Feature-Space OOD Detection (No Retraining, Most Principled)                                                                  
                                                                                                                                           
  Idea: Use the CNN's own learned features to detect whether an input "looks like" training data.
                                                                                                                                           
  How:                                                                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                           
  1. At setup time: Run all training images through the model, extract the 256-dim feature vector (output of GAP, before dropout/FC).      
  Compute the mean and covariance per class.                                                                                               
  2. At inference time: Extract the same 256-dim vector for the new image. Compute its Mahalanobis distance to the nearest class centroid. 
  3. If the distance exceeds a threshold → reject as OOD.                                                                                  
                                                                                                                                           
    Model forward pass (modified to expose features)                                                                                       
           ↓                                                                                                                               
    256-dim feature vector                                                                                                                 
           ↓                                                                                                                               
    Mahalanobis distance to nearest class centroid                                                                                         
           ↓                                                                                                                               
    Distance > threshold? → OOD → Reject
    Distance ≤ threshold? → In-distribution → Show prediction                                                                              
                                                                                                                                           
  Pros: Mathematically principled, uses the model's own representation, no retraining needed                                               
  Cons: Requires saving class centroids/covariances from training data, slightly more complex to implement                                 
  ──────                                                                                                                                   
  ## Fix 5 — Monte Carlo Dropout (No Retraining)                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                           
  Idea: Run inference multiple times with dropout enabled and measure how much predictions vary.                                           
                                                                                                                                           
  How:

  • Run the same image through the model ~20-30 times                                                                                      
  • Each pass produces slightly different predictions due to dropout randomness                                                            
  • If predictions are consistent → model is confident → trust it
  • If predictions vary wildly → model is uncertain → reject                                                                               
                                                                                                                                           
    model.train()  # keeps dropout active                                                                                                  
    predictions = []                                                                                                                       
    for _ in range(30):

    stacked = torch.stack(predictions)                                                                                                     
    mean_pred = stacked.mean(dim=0)                                                                                                        
    variance = stacked.var(dim=0).sum().item()                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                           
    if variance > threshold:
        # High disagreement → uncertain → reject                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                           
  Pros: No retraining, no extra data, captures genuine model uncertainty                                                                   
  Cons: 30× slower inference, still not guaranteed to catch all OOD inputs
